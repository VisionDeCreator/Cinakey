The app brings script writing, references, video generation, 3D staging and timeline
editing together in one browser workspace.

Start with a standalone project, or create a Series when you want multiple episodes
to share characters, locations, props and other resources. Episodes keep their own
scripts, generation jobs and edits.

Inside a project, write your script and upload images or reuse existing assets as
references. The Studio workspace keeps your script, references, generation history,
viewer and timeline connected. You can use 3D staging to plan camera movement and
scene timing before generation.

When footage is ready, preview it and add the parts you want to your timeline.
Previewing a generation does not replace your saved edit.

You can resize the workspace panels with their dividers. Panel buttons let you
collapse the library, inspector, generations and timeline, and the layout is
remembered in your browser.

If you are unsure about a step, open Help and support to ask a question or request
help from a person.
