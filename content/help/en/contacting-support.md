Open Help and support in the signed-in app to ask a product question, browse help
articles or return to an earlier conversation.

The AI assistant uses published help articles to answer product questions. When
an answer includes a help source, open that source to read the article.

To get help from a person, choose Speak to a human. Your conversation and its
history stay together, so you do not need to repeat the whole issue. Our team
replies in the same chat. You can add more details and come back later to check
for a response; requesting a human does not guarantee an immediate reply.

You can attach PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF files. Each message supports up to three
files, with a maximum size of 3 MB per file. Use screenshots or documents to help
the support team understand the issue. The AI assistant does not inspect the
contents of uploaded images or PDFs.

The interface supports English, French, Spanish, Korean, Japanese and Simplified
Chinese. Use the conversation language selector to choose how messages are shown.
Messages can be translated automatically, and Show original lets you read the
original text. If a translation is unavailable, you can retry it.

If a conversation has been resolved and you need more help, reopen it to continue.
