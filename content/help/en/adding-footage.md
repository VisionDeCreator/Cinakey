title: Adding generated footage to your timeline
category: Studio
published: true
---
Preview your generated footage in the Generation panel. Mark the source range you
want to use, then choose Add to timeline to insert it.

You can also drag a generation or library media onto a timeline lane. Dropping a
local file onto a lane uploads and inserts it.

When you insert footage, the app splits clips at the insertion point and shifts
later clips on that lane. Other lanes keep their timing. One undo restores the
previous edit.

To add several videos, select them in the order you want and use Add selected
videos. To change the source of an existing clip, use Replace clip source.

Previewing a generation does not replace your saved edit. Adding footage to the
timeline is a separate action.
